`inlang machine translate` sends at most 4 batch requests at once, or 16 for a provider that translates one text per request (the community service sends 6 of them at a time), and keeps translating unrelated messages when an individual text fails. A rate-limited request (429) is sent again up to 3 times, after the provider's `Retry-After` or 2, 4 and 8 seconds, waiting at most a minute in total; other failures aren't retried. The community service instead sends a request up to 3 times on a 429, a server error or a network failure, after 5 and 10 seconds. Authentication, billing, and service failures, and rate limits that outlast the retries, stop new requests. It still saves the translations that came back, says how many, and exits with code 1; run it again to translate the rest. If that save fails, it reports both why translation stopped and why the save failed. A new message gets a language only when all its translations came back; a message that exists keeps those that did.
